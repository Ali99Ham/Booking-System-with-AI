# Solar Club Germany — Club facts

## Basics

- Location: Oldenburg, Germany. Free parking on site.
- Courts: 5 indoor, climate-controlled, panoramic glass padel courts.
- Hours: every day 6:00 AM to midnight. Slots are 60 minutes and start on the hour.
- Contact: reception@solarclub.de — but bookings are fastest right here on the phone.

## Prices

- Off-peak (6 AM–5 PM): €30 per hour. Peak (5 PM–midnight): €45 per hour.
- Price is per court, not per player. Up to 4 players per court.
- Payment: card or EC-Karte / Apple Pay at reception on arrival. No deposit needed.
- Cancellation: free up to 4 hours before the slot; later than that, half the court fee.

## Equipment & rules

- Rackets €6 each to rent, balls included with every booking.
- Non-marking shoes are required — no black soles. Padel or tennis shoes are fine.
- Towels and showers included; lockers free, bring your own padlock or buy one for €4.

## Coaching & classes

- Intro classes Saturday mornings, €25 per person, rackets included.
- Private coaching €60 per hour with head coach, €50 with academy coaches.
- Kids academy (ages 7–14) Tuesdays and Thursdays 5–6 PM, €90 per month.

## Membership

- No membership required — everyone can book.
- Solar Club Card: €120 per month, includes 6 off-peak hours, 15% off peak bookings, and priority booking 14 days ahead (non-members book 7 days ahead).

## Events

- Friday night social tournaments, 8 PM, €25 per player including one drink. All levels, sign up at reception or by phone.
- Courts can be booked for private events or corporate sessions — reception takes the request and the events manager calls back within a day.
